How the estimate works

Today's and tomorrow's prices are the published Nord Pool day-ahead prices for SE4, every 15 minutes, via elprisetjustnu.se. Tomorrow's arrive around 13:00. Further ahead the app estimates: it starts from the past week's prices, then adjusts for the wind and temperature forecast from MET Norway and for weekends. Calm, cold days are expensive; windy days can push prices close to zero. Each estimated day is spread over the usual daily pattern of the last four weeks.
